---

## 🎯 Recommended Testing Flow (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Homepage
- Open `index.html`
- Scroll through hero, featured courses, stats

### Step 2: Login as Learner
- Click **Login** (top right)
- Use: `learner@demo.ccrest.in` / `demo123`
- Or use the "Google" quick login button

### Step 3: Enrol in a Course
- Go to **Courses**
- Click a **FREE** course → instant access
- Click a **PAID** course → try the full checkout (Razorpay or Stripe)

### Step 4: Course Experience
- Open any course detail page
- Click lessons to open video player (YouTube embed)
- Complete lessons → watch progress update
- Finish the course → get your **Certificate**

### Step 5: Switch Roles
- Logout and login as:
  - Teacher → `teacher@demo.ccrest.in`
  - Admin → `admin@ccrest.in`
