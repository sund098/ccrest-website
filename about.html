About CCREST

We are India's leading independent centre for professional education in clinical research, dedicated to building the next generation of skilled life sciences professionals.

Our Mission
To deliver high-quality, accessible, and industry-relevant training programmes using the best available free tools and open-source platforms. We believe professional education should not be locked behind expensive paywalls.
